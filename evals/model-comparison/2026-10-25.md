# Codex and Claude on the eval suite

Run on 2026-10-25 against the standard cases: 60 snip titles (snip summary v1) and 510 snip-start taps (snip start v1). Production writes titles with Codex at low effort and leaves the start picker off, so its starts come from the fixed rule.

## Result

- **Titles: a tie.** Claude Opus 5.5 and Codex at high effort both match the owner's point on 70% of titles, and where they disagree each wins 5 cases. Both score 5 to 10 points above Codex at low effort, depending on the judge, but no title gap passes a significance test at 60 cases, and two runs of low effort alone differ on 10 cases. Both judges rank the models the same way.
- **Starts: Codex beats Claude.** Claude needs an edit on more taps than Codex at either effort, and it starts snips earliest. Every model does worse than the fixed rule production uses, so the start picker should stay off.
- **Guard failures.** Codex at low effort failed the names guard twice on 1 to 3 titles per run, each time on an ordinary capitalized word. Codex at high effort and Claude never did.

## How it ran

- **Codex** ran through the app's own adapter (`codex exec` with a read-only sandbox and the CLI's default model, gpt-6-astra), at low and at high reasoning effort. Low effort ran twice: today, which is the production row, and an earlier run read from the eval cache (starts from 2026-10-21, titles from 2026-10-23).
- **Claude Opus 5.5** (`claude-opus-5-5`) answered in a Claude Code session, through subagents that read the exact prompts. Its answers went back through the same prompt code, validation, retry, and scoring as Codex's.
- **Titles** were scored by the eval's judge (`judge_v1`, run by Codex at low effort) and again by a blind Claude judge with the same prompt and no model names.

## Caveats

- **Judges may favor their own model.** The Codex judge marks Codex titles 5 to 9 points higher than the Claude judge does, and Claude's titles 2 points higher. The ranking is the same under both judges, and they agree on 90% of pass or fail calls.
- **Both models had tools.** Codex runs with a read-only shell. Claude's subagents had a shell too, and some used it to check title and summary lengths, and once capitalized words, before answering. That mostly helps pass the guard on the first try, and the retry catches the rest.
- **Repeated start prompts.** Several taps produce the same picker prompt. Claude answered each of the 310 distinct prompts once; Codex answered all 510 taps.
- **The names guard misfires.** Of Claude's 14 first-try rejections, 8 were Title Case words and 1 was "Solana", which the transcript doesn't contain. The other 5 were false alarms: the guard joins title and summary with a space, so the summary's first word reads as mid-sentence, and it misses a sentence that ends in a straight quote.
- **Latency.** Claude's answers were replayed from files, so its latency wasn't measured.
